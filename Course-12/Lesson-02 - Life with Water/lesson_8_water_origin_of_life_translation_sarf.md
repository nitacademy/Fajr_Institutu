# 3. التَّرْجَمَةُ الإِنْجِلِيزِيَّةُ الْكَامِلَةُ
## Complete Natural English Translation

Water is the origin and secret of life. It is the primary element that constitutes every living cell; therefore, there can be no life without water.

Allah Most High says:

> “We made every living thing from water. Will they not then believe?”  
> **[Sūrat al-Anbiyāʾ: 30]**

Water is also extremely important for every form of plant life, confirming the statement of Allah Most High:

> “He sent down water from the sky, and through it We brought forth diverse varieties of plants.”  
> **[Sūrat Ṭā Hā: 53]**

Water is likewise the origin of every form of animal life. Allah says:

> “Allah created every moving creature from water.”  
> **[Sūrat al-Nūr: 45]**

Some scientists define life as a water-based phenomenon because there is not a single living organism that can survive without water.

It is true that some organisms can withstand dryness for a long period, but they do so only while they are dormant and inactive. They remain covered with protective layers that prevent them from drying out and dying.

Nevertheless, there is not a single living organism that can grow and reproduce without water.

# 5. الأَبْوَابُ السِّتَّةُ لِلثُّلَاثِيِّ الْمُجَرَّدِ
## The Six Bābs of the Triliteral Unaugmented Verb

> Only a verb that is genuinely **ثُلَاثِيًّا مُجَرَّدًا** in the form being studied belongs to one of the six bābs. Derived verbs such as **عَرَّفَ** and **اسْتَطَاعَ** do not belong to the six mujarrad bābs in the forms used in this lesson.

| Traditional bāb | Pattern | Standard example | Recognition |
|---|---|---|---|
| بَابُ نَصَرَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعُلُ | نَصَرَ يَنْصُرُ | fatḥah in the past middle radical; ḍammah in the present |
| بَابُ ضَرَبَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعِلُ | ضَرَبَ يَضْرِبُ | fatḥah in the past; kasrah in the present |
| بَابُ فَتَحَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعَلُ | فَتَحَ يَفْتَحُ | fatḥah in both past and present |
| بَابُ سَمِعَ | فَعِلَ يَفْعَلُ | سَمِعَ يَسْمَعُ | kasrah in the past; fatḥah in the present |
| بَابُ كَرُمَ | فَعُلَ يَفْعُلُ | كَرُمَ يَكْرُمُ | ḍammah in both past and present |
| بَابُ حَسِبَ | فَعِلَ يَفْعِلُ | حَسِبَ يَحْسِبُ | kasrah in both past and present |

> Different ṣarf books sometimes number these bābs differently. Memorize the **name and vowel pattern**, not only the number.

# 7. مُلَاحَظَاتٌ نَحْوِيَّةٌ وَصَرْفِيَّةٌ
## Important Grammar and Morphology Notes

## 1. **فَلَا حَيَاةَ بِلَا مَاءٍ**

This means:

> There is no life without water.

The first **لَا** is **لَا النَّافِيَةُ لِلْجِنْسِ**, which completely negates the existence of its noun:

- **لَا حَيَاةَ** = there is absolutely no life.

In **بِلَا مَاءٍ**, the second **لَا** is part of the expression meaning **without**.

## 2. **وَجَعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمَاءِ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ حَيٍّ**

- **جَعَلْنَا** = We made
- **نَا** = We
- **كُلَّ شَيْءٍ** = every thing
- **حَيٍّ** = living

The adjective **حَيٍّ** agrees with **شَيْءٍ** in indefiniteness and grammatical case.

## 3. **فَأَخْرَجْنَا بِهِ أَزْوَاجًا**

The pronoun **ـهِ** in **بِهِ** refers to the water:

> Through it, We brought forth varieties.

In this context, **أَزْوَاجًا** also means kinds, varieties, or categories.

## 4. **يُعَرِّفُونَ** versus **يَعْرِفُونَ**

| Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| يَعْرِفُونَ | they know or recognize |
| يُعَرِّفُونَ | they define or explain |

The lesson says:

> **بَعْضُ الْعُلَمَاءِ يُعَرِّفُونَ الْحَيَاةَ**

“Some scientists define life.” Therefore this is Form II **عَرَّفَ يُعَرِّفُ**, not the Form I verb **عَرَفَ يَعْرِفُ**.

## 5. Non-human plural agreement

The sentence says:

> **بَعْضُ الْكَائِنَاتِ تَسْتَطِيعُ تَحَمُّلَ الْجَفَافِ**

Although **الْكَائِنَاتُ** is plural, it is a non-human plural. Non-human plurals are commonly treated as feminine singular in Arabic agreement. This explains:

- **تَسْتَطِيعُ** rather than a masculine plural form
- **وَهِيَ كَامِنَةٌ**
- **مُتَدَثِّرَةٌ**

## 6. **تَحْمِيهَا**

This word contains:

- **تَحْمِي** = it protects
- **هَا** = them/it, referring to the organisms

The subject is **أَغْطِيَةٌ**, a non-human plural, so the verb appears as feminine singular:

> **أَغْطِيَةٌ تَحْمِيهَا** = coverings that protect them.

## 7. **مِنْ أَنْ تَجِفَّ حَتَّى تَمُوتَ**

Both verbs are in the subjunctive:

- **أَنْ تَجِفَّ** = that they dry up
- **حَتَّى تَمُوتَ** = until they die

## 8. Weak and doubled verbs

### **حَمَى — يَحْمِي**

This is a defective weak verb:

- Past: حَمَى
- Present: يَحْمِي
- Command: اِحْمِ

The final weak letter is removed in the command.

### **جَفَّ — يَجِفُّ**

This is a doubled verb because the second and third root letters are both **ف**:

- Root: ج ف ف
- Past: جَفَّ
- Present: يَجِفُّ
- Command: جِفَّ

# 8. خُطَّةُ الْحِفْظِ وَالإِتْقَانِ فِي يَوْمٍ إِلَى ثَلَاثَةِ أَيَّامٍ
## A One-to-Three-Day Memorization and Mastery Plan

> Strong understanding and short-term mastery are achievable in one to three days. For long-term retention, review the lesson again after seven days and after thirty days.

## One-Day Intensive Plan

### Session 1 — Understand the main ideas, 35 minutes

Divide the passage into five ideas:

1. Water is the basis of every living cell.
2. Plant life depends upon water.
3. Animal life also originates from water.
4. No living organism can truly live without water.
5. Dormant organisms may endure dryness, but they cannot grow or reproduce without water.

Read each part aloud twice and explain it in English without looking.

### Session 2 — Memorize the Qur’anic evidence, 30 minutes

Study these three proofs separately:

1. **وَجَعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمَاءِ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ حَيٍّ**
2. **فَأَخْرَجْنَا بِهِ أَزْوَاجًا مِنْ نَبَاتٍ شَتَّى**
3. **وَاللهُ خَلَقَ كُلَّ دَابَّةٍ مِنْ مَاءٍ**

For each one:

- read it five times,
- state its English meaning,
- identify the word connected with water,
- explain how it supports the composition.

Group the vocabulary by subject:
