### Exercise (4): Answer briefly:
1. **Why do some scientists say that life is a water phenomenon?**
   * Because not a single living organism can live, grow, or reproduce without water.
2. **How can some living organisms tolerate drought without water?**
   * They do so by entering a dormant state with no activity, protected by coverings that shield them from drying out.
3. **In what things does the percentage of water decrease significantly?**
   * In seeds, hooves, horns, and certain desert animals.
4. **What is the percentage of water in your body (as a human)?**
   * About two-thirds of the human body is water (approximately 65%).
5. **When does the story of water begin with a human being?**
   * It begins from the moment they are a drop of fluid (zygote) swimming in water, and then as a fetus in their mother's womb.
6. **Where do humans get 86% of their water intake requirements?**
   * From two main sources: drinking water and liquids at 47%, and solid foods (such as meat and vegetables) at 39%.
7. **What do you feel if your body loses about 1% of its water?**
   * The person feels severe thirst (hydration deficit).
8. **What do you feel if your body loses about 5% of its water?**
   * Their throat and skin dry up, and they suffer from total physical collapse.
9. **What is the danger of an excessive increase of water in the body?**
   * It causes nausea, high blood pressure, mental confusion, seizures, coma, and eventually death.
10. **Can wars break out because of water? Explain.**
    * Yes, history has recorded many battles fought over water resources, and entire civilizations have perished due to losing access to it.
