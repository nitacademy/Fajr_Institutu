### **Pre-Reading Questions & Model Answers**

**1. What advice does a mother usually give her daughter just before marriage in your society?**
* **Answer:** A mother usually advises her daughter to respect and obey her husband, keep his secrets, maintain a clean and welcoming home, prepare his meals, treat his family with kindness, and remain patient through life's ups and downs.

**2. What advice does a father usually give his daughter just before marriage in your society?**
* **Answer:** A father usually advises his daughter to be a source of comfort and tranquility for her husband, to consciously step up to her new responsibilities, and to understand that marriage is not just a leisure trip, but a partnership built on mutual sacrifice and family-building.

**3. Some girls get married simply to escape the restrictions imposed by their parents. Do you agree with this approach? Why or why not?**
* **Answer:** I do not agree. Parental restrictions are generally meant for the safety and well-being of their daughters. Furthermore, marriage does not mean absolute freedom; it brings a completely new set of duties and boundaries that can often be more demanding than those in her parents' home.

**4. Where does a young woman carry greater responsibility: in her father's house or in her own married home?**
* **Answer:** She carries a much greater responsibility in her own married home. There, she becomes fully responsible for managing an entire household, raising children, budgeting, cooking, and keeping the home organized.

**5. Why do you think divorce rates are increasingly high among young couples nowadays?**
* **Answer:** Divorce rates are high primarily due to a lack of deep understanding regarding the realities of marriage. Some young couples view it merely as a source of fun or comfort. When faced with daily challenges, a lack of patience and an inability to adapt to conflicts often lead to separation.

**6. What material things does a wife like to secure for her home and focus on deeply? In your opinion, do these things actually bring happiness?**
* **Answer:** A wife often likes to secure beautiful furniture, modern clothing, jewelry, and elegant decor, focusing heavily on cleanliness and organization. However, material things alone do not bring true happiness if emotional stability, peace of mind, and mutual respect are missing.

**7. In your opinion, what are the core elements that truly achieve marital happiness?**
* **Answer:** True marital happiness is achieved through affection and mercy, mutual respect, cooperation in carrying life's responsibilities, patience during difficult times, and healthy, open communication between both partners.
