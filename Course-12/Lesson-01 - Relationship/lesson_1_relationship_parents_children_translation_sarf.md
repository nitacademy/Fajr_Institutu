# 3. التَّرْجَمَةُ الإِنْجِلِيزِيَّةُ الْكَامِلَةُ
## Complete English Translation

### The Relationship Between Parents and Children

The relationship between parents and children is an issue that has occupied all people in every time and place. However, the Noble Qur’an has clearly defined the sound principles that govern the relationship between parents and children, so that none of the limits established by Allah Most High are transgressed in the practice of this relationship.

Here is Luqman the Wise, who presents to every father the highest example of fatherhood—fatherhood that deeply understands the rights a son has over his father. He fulfilled his duty toward his son in the best manner when he advised him.

The first thing with which he equipped his son was the sound creed, purified from associating partners with Allah. Polytheism is a tremendous wrongdoing because it equates the Creator, the Possessor of all blessings, with one who creates nothing and from whom no blessing comes at all.

Luqman urges his son to remain conscious of Allah in his words and deeds, in their smallest matters before their greatest ones, because every person is held accountable for what he earns.

Luqman affectionately addresses his son and commands him to perform the prayer sincerely, so that he may connect himself with his Creator. He also instructs him to follow the path of practically applying faith: to enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and remain patient over whatever befalls him in the cause of Allah, because Allah has made these duties obligatory.

Luqman guides his son toward a collection of good qualities and actions that earn for him the love of Allah and the love of Allah’s servants. These include humility toward people, moderation in his walk, and lowering his voice. Allah does not love the arrogant, the conceited, or those who boast about themselves; and the most unpleasant of sounds is the voice of donkeys.

Allah Most High says:

> “And when Luqman said to his son, while advising him, ‘My dear son, do not associate anything with Allah. Indeed, associating partners with Allah is surely a tremendous wrongdoing.’”

And He says:

> “My dear son, establish the prayer, enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and remain patient over whatever befalls you. Indeed, these are matters requiring firm resolve. Do not turn your cheek away from people in arrogance, and do not walk upon the earth boastfully. Indeed, Allah does not love any conceited boaster. Be moderate in your walk and lower your voice. Indeed, the harshest of sounds is surely the voice of donkeys.”

# 5. الأَبْوَابُ السِّتَّةُ لِلثُّلَاثِيِّ المُجَرَّدِ
## The Six Bābs of the Triliteral Unaugmented Verb

> **Important:** Only a genuinely **ثُلَاثِيٌّ مُجَرَّدٌ** verb belongs to one of these six bābs.  
> Verbs such as **حَدَّدَ، تَعَدَّى، زَوَّدَ، نَادَى، أَرْشَدَ، صَعَّرَ** are **مَزِيدٌ فِيهِ** (augmented/derived), so they do **not** belong to one of the six bābs in the exact form used in the lesson.

| Traditional name | Pattern | Example | Basic identification |
|---|---|---|---|
| بَابُ نَصَرَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعُلُ | نَصَرَ يَنْصُرُ | past middle vowel fatḥah; present middle vowel ḍammah |
| بَابُ ضَرَبَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعِلُ | ضَرَبَ يَضْرِبُ | past middle vowel fatḥah; present middle vowel kasrah |
| بَابُ فَتَحَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعَلُ | فَتَحَ يَفْتَحُ | past middle vowel fatḥah; present middle vowel fatḥah |
| بَابُ سَمِعَ | فَعِلَ يَفْعَلُ | سَمِعَ يَسْمَعُ | past middle vowel kasrah; present middle vowel fatḥah |
| بَابُ كَرُمَ | فَعُلَ يَفْعُلُ | كَرُمَ يَكْرُمُ | past middle vowel ḍammah; present middle vowel ḍammah |
| بَابُ حَسِبَ | فَعِلَ يَفْعِلُ | حَسِبَ يَحْسِبُ | past middle vowel kasrah; present middle vowel kasrah |

> Some books number the six bābs differently. Memorize the **name and vowel pattern**, not only the number.

## 7. مُلَاحَظَاتٌ صَرْفِيَّةٌ مُهِمَّةٌ
## Important Morphology Notes

1. **وَصَلَ يَصِلُ** is a **مِثَالٌ وَاوِيٌّ** verb. The initial **و** drops in the present and command:
   - وَصَلَ → يَصِلُ → صِلْ

2. **نَهَى يَنْهَى** is a **فِعْلٌ نَاقِصٌ**. Its final weak letter is removed in the command:
   - نَهَى → يَنْهَى → اِنْهَ

3. **حَثَّ** and **غَضَّ** are **مُضَاعَفٌ** verbs:
   - حَثَّ → يَحُثُّ → حُثَّ
   - غَضَّ → يَغُضُّ → غُضَّ  
   The Qur’anic wording uses the separated form **وَاغْضُضْ**.

4. A verb may appear in the composition in a changed grammatical form:
   - **شَغَلَتْ**: past tense, feminine singular, because **قَضِيَّةٌ** is feminine.
   - **لِيَصِلَ**: present subjunctive after **لِـ** expressing purpose.
   - **يَسْلُكَ**: present subjunctive after **أَنْ**.
   - **تُصَعِّرْ**: present jussive after **لَا النَّاهِيَةُ**.
   - **وَاقْصِدْ، وَاغْضُضْ، وَأْمُرْ، وَانْهَ**: commands.

5. In this lesson, the mujarrad verbs belong mainly to:
   - **بَابُ نَصَرَ:** حَثَّ، سَلَكَ، أَمَرَ، غَضَّ
   - **بَابُ ضَرَبَ:** وَعَظَ، كَسَبَ، وَصَلَ، قَصَدَ
   - **بَابُ فَتَحَ:** شَغَلَ، نَهَى  
   There are no clear lesson verbs from **بَابُ سَمِعَ، بَابُ كَرُمَ،** or **بَابُ حَسِبَ**.

# 8. خُطَّةُ الحِفْظِ وَالإِتْقَانِ فِي يَوْمٍ إِلَى ثَلَاثَةِ أَيَّامٍ
## A 1–3 Day Memorization and Mastery Plan

> You can achieve strong short-term mastery in one to three days. Long-term retention still requires brief reviews after 1 week and 1 month.

## One-Day Intensive Plan

### Session 1 — 45 minutes
- Read the full Arabic passage aloud three times.
- Read each inline Arabic-English sentence twice.
- Divide the passage into six meaning-units:
  1. The relationship and Qur’anic limits
  2. Luqman as the model father
  3. Correct belief and the danger of shirk
  4. Awareness of Allah and accountability
  5. Prayer and practical faith
  6. Humility, walking, and lowering the voice

### Session 2 — 45 minutes
- Memorize the sixteen verbs in groups of four.
- For every verb, say aloud:
  - past
  - present
  - command
  - verbal noun
  - bāb/form
  - the exact form used in the composition

Example:

> وَعَظَ — يَعِظُ — عِظْ — وَعْظٌ — بَابُ ضَرَبَ — وَعَظَهُ / يَعِظُهُ

### Session 3 — 30 minutes
- Cover the English and translate the Arabic orally.
- Cover the Arabic and reconstruct the key Arabic sentence from the English.
- Write the ten most important vocabulary words from memory.

### Session 4 — 20 minutes
- Recite the complete meaning without looking.
- Test yourself on all mujarrad verbs:
  - Is it بَابُ نَصَرَ, ضَرَبَ, or فَتَحَ?
  - Why? Check the vowel of the middle radical in the present tense.

## Best Memorization Technique

Use **active recall**, not repeated passive reading:

1. Look at **شَغَلَ**.
2. Hide the row.
3. Say: **يَشْغَلُ، اِشْغَلْ، شُغْلٌ، بَابُ فَتَحَ**.
4. Recall its composition form: **شَغَلَتِ النَّاسَ**.
5. Check your answer.
6. Repeat only the items you missed.

Create flashcards with:

- **Front:** Arabic verb or vocabulary word
- **Back:** English meaning, conjugation, bāb/form, and the sentence from the lesson

### Final mastery check

You have mastered the lesson when you can:

- [ ] Read the passage smoothly without stopping.
- [ ] Explain every sentence in English.
- [ ] Translate at least 80% without looking.
- [ ] Conjugate all sixteen verbs.
- [ ] Separate mujarrad verbs from derived verbs.
- [ ] Name the correct bāb for every mujarrad verb.
- [ ] Recite or accurately explain the Qur’anic advice contained in the lesson.
