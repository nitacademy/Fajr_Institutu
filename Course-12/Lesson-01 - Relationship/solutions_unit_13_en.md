# Solution Manual for Unit 13 Exercises: The Relationship Between Parents and Children

## Lesson: "Pre-Reading"

### 1. From reading the title, what do you expect the text to discuss?
* **Answer:** The text will address the proper foundations of the relationship between parents and children as outlined in the Holy Qur'an.

### 2. What are the rights of parents over children and children over parents in your opinion?
* **Answer:** 
  * **Rights of Parents:** Dutifulness (Birr), obedience in good matters, and profound respect.
  * **Rights of Children:** Good upbringing (tarbiyah), sound advice, financial provision, and affection.

### 3. Do you know a story of a father who gave his son a good sermon in the Qur'an? What did he say to him?
* **Answer:** Yes, the story of Luqman the Wise. He said to him: "O my son, do not associate anything with Allah, establish prayer, enjoin what is right, and forbid what is wrong."

### 4. Do you know a story of a son who was dutiful to his father mentioned in the Qur'an? What is the father's name? And who is the son?
* **Answer:** Yes, the father is **Ibrahim** (peace be upon him), and the son is **Isma'il** (peace be upon him).

### 5. Do you know a story of a son who did not listen to his father's advice and died by drowning? What is the father's name? And who is the son?
* **Answer:** Yes, the father is **Nuh** (peace be upon him), and the son is **Kan'an** (the son of Nuh).

### Exercise (4): Answer briefly:

* **1. How did the Qur'an define the relationship between parents and children?** It clearly defined the sound foundations governing the relationship so that neither side exceeds the boundaries of Allah.
* **2. Why was polytheism (shirk) a great injustice?** Because it equates the Creator, who is the giver of all blessings, with entities that cannot create and have no inherent blessings to offer.
* **3. Mention four things Luqman commanded his son to do:** Establishing prayer, enjoining what is right, forbidding what is wrong, and bearing patiently whatever befalls him.
* **4. Mention two things he forbade him from doing:** Arrogance (turning his cheek away from people) and walking insolently upon the earth.
* **5. To what did Luqman compare the most disagreeable of voices?** To the voice of donkeys.
* **6. Who was given as a supreme example of fatherhood?** Luqman the Wise.
* **7. Who was given as a supreme example of childhood/sonship?** Isma'il (peace be upon him).
* **8. Provide evidence from the text showing that Ibrahim and Isma'il were Muslims:** The verse: {رَبَّنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِنَا أُمَّةً مُسْلِمَةً لَكَ} ("Our Lord, and make us Muslims [in submission] to You and from our descendants a Muslim nation [in submission] to You").
* **9. How did Ibrahim prove he was obedient to Allah?** He refused to follow his father and his people in their devotion to idol worship.
* **10. Why did Nuh disassociate from his son?** Because his son was a disbeliever whose conduct was unrighteous, as clarified by Allah.
