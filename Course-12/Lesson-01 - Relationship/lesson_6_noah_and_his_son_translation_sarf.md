# 2. التَّرْجَمَةُ الإِنْجِلِيزِيَّةُ الْكَامِلَةُ
## Complete Natural English Translation

Parental love for children does not mean that parents should accept their children with all their faults, overlook their mistakes, and search for excuses for those mistakes in the name of tender parental affection. The Noble Qur’an rejects this idea in the account of Noah—peace be upon him—and his son.

Allah Most High relates that Noah told the believers to board the Ark in the name of Allah, under whose command it would sail and come to rest. As the Ark carried them through waves resembling mountains, Noah called to his son, who had separated himself from them, and urged him to board with the believers rather than remain with the disbelievers.

His son answered that he would seek refuge on a mountain that would protect him from the water. Noah explained that no one could provide protection that day from Allah’s decree except the one upon whom Allah showed mercy. A wave then came between father and son, and the son became one of those who were drowned.

The earth was commanded to swallow its water, and the sky was commanded to withhold its rain. The water subsided, the decree was fulfilled, and the Ark settled upon Mount al-Jūdī. Destruction was declared for the wrongdoing people.

Noah then called upon his Lord, saying that his son belonged to his family and that Allah’s promise was true. Allah explained that the son was not counted among Noah’s true family because his conduct was unrighteous. Noah was instructed not to ask about something of which he had no knowledge and was warned against acting ignorantly.

Noah responded by seeking refuge in Allah from asking about that of which he had no knowledge. He acknowledged that unless Allah forgave him and showed him mercy, he would be among the losers.

In this way, the Noble Qur’an explained the relationship between parents and children fourteen centuries ago. It assigned each person his rights, defined his responsibilities, and required everyone to practice this relationship through cooperation in righteousness and piety, abandonment of sin and aggression, encouragement of what is right, and prevention of what is wrong.

Through these principles the family becomes happy and prosperous. Parents and children cooperate in placing good building blocks in the construction of the best community ever brought forth for humanity. Allah Most High describes this community as one that enjoins what is right, forbids what is wrong, and believes in Allah.

# 4. الأَبْوَابُ السِّتَّةُ لِلثُّلَاثِيِّ الْمُجَرَّدِ
## The Six Bābs of the Triliteral Unaugmented Verb

> A verb belongs to one of the six bābs only when the form being studied is genuinely **ثُلَاثِيًّا مُجَرَّدًا**.  
> Derived forms such as **أَقْلَعَ، اسْتَوَى، نَادَى، بَيَّنَ** do not belong to the six bābs in the forms used in this lesson.

| Traditional bāb | Pattern | Standard example | Recognition |
|---|---|---|---|
| بَابُ نَصَرَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعُلُ | نَصَرَ يَنْصُرُ | fatḥah in the past; ḍammah in the present |
| بَابُ ضَرَبَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعِلُ | ضَرَبَ يَضْرِبُ | fatḥah in the past; kasrah in the present |
| بَابُ فَتَحَ | فَعَلَ يَفْعَلُ | فَتَحَ يَفْتَحُ | fatḥah in both past and present |
| بَابُ سَمِعَ | فَعِلَ يَفْعَلُ | سَمِعَ يَسْمَعُ | kasrah in the past; fatḥah in the present |
| بَابُ كَرُمَ | فَعُلَ يَفْعُلُ | كَرُمَ يَكْرُمُ | ḍammah in both past and present |
| بَابُ حَسِبَ | فَعِلَ يَفْعِلُ | حَسِبَ يَحْسِبُ | kasrah in both past and present |

> Different ṣarf books may number the bābs differently. Memorize the **traditional name and vowel pattern**, not merely the number.

### Important correction concerning **سَآوِي**

The worksheet presents a conjugation resembling:

> آوَى — يُؤْوِي — آوِ — إِيوَاءٌ

That is the **Form IV** verb **آوَى يُؤْوِي**, meaning **to shelter or give refuge to someone**.

The Qur’anic form **سَآوِي إِلَى جَبَلٍ**, however, comes from:

> **أَوَى — يَأْوِي — اِئْوِ**

It means:

> **to seek refuge or resort to a place**

Therefore, the verb used in the composition is **Form I, بَابُ ضَرَبَ**, not Form IV.

Compare:

- **أَوَى الرَّجُلُ إِلَى الْجَبَلِ** = The man sought refuge on the mountain.
- **آوَى الرَّجُلُ الضَّيْفَ** = The man gave shelter to the guest.

| بَيَّنَ | بَيَّنَ | يُبَيِّنُ | بَيِّنْ | تَبْيِينٌ / بَيَانٌ | to explain; clarify; make evident | **Not one of the six:** Form II, بَابُ التَّفْعِيلِ — فَعَّلَ يُفَعِّلُ | **وَهَكَذَا بَيَّنَ الْقُرْآنُ الْكَرِيمُ** = in this way the Noble Qur’an explained |
| تَسْعَدُ | سَعِدَ | يَسْعَدُ | اسْعَدْ | سَعَادَةٌ | to be happy; prosper; attain happiness | **بَابُ سَمِعَ** — فَعِلَ يَفْعَلُ | **وَبِذَلِكَ تَسْعَدُ الْأُسْرَةُ** = through that the family becomes happy |

## Summary: Mujarrad Verbs by Bāb

### بَابُ نَصَرَ — فَعَلَ يَفْعُلُ

- **حَالَ — يَحُولُ**
- **عَاذَ — يَعُوذُ**

### بَابُ ضَرَبَ — فَعَلَ يَفْعِلُ

- **أَوَى — يَأْوِي**
- **غَاضَ — يَغِيضُ**

### بَابُ سَمِعَ — فَعِلَ يَفْعَلُ

- **رَكِبَ — يَرْكَبُ**
- **رَحِمَ — يَرْحَمُ**
- **سَعِدَ — يَسْعَدُ**

### Derived Forms — الْمَزِيدُ فِيهِ

- **Form II — التَّفْعِيل:** بَيَّنَ
- **Form III — الْمُفَاعَلَة:** نَادَى
- **Form IV — الْإِفْعَال:** أَقْلَعَ
- **Form VIII — الِافْتِعَال:** اسْتَوَى

> The table contains no clear examples from **بَابُ فَتَحَ، بَابُ كَرُمَ،** or **بَابُ حَسِبَ**.

# 6. مُلَاحَظَاتٌ نَحْوِيَّةٌ وَصَرْفِيَّةٌ
## Important Grammar and Morphology Notes

## 1. **ارْكَبُوا** and **ارْكَبْ**

Both are commands from **رَكِبَ يَرْكَبُ**:

- **ارْكَبُوا** = all of you board
- **ارْكَبْ** = you, one male, board

The passage first addresses the believers collectively and then addresses Noah’s son individually.

---

## 2. Feminine commands: **ابْلَعِي** and **أَقْلِعِي**

The earth and sky are grammatically feminine:

- **يَا أَرْضُ ابْلَعِي** = O earth, swallow!
- **يَا سَمَاءُ أَقْلِعِي** = O sky, withhold!

The ending **ـِي** marks the feminine singular command.

---

## 3. Passive verbs in the verse

The passage uses several passive past-tense verbs:

| Passive verb | Active base | English |
|---|---|---|
| قِيلَ | قَالَ | it was said |
| غِيضَ | غَاضَ | it was made to subside / it subsided |
| قُضِيَ | قَضَى | it was decided/completed |

The object or result is emphasized rather than the doer.

---

## 4. **اسْتَوَتْ**

The base verb is:

> **اسْتَوَى — يَسْتَوِي**

The passage says:

> **وَاسْتَوَتْ عَلَى الْجُودِيِّ**

The feminine ending **ـتْ** refers to the Ark, **السَّفِينَةُ**, which is grammatically feminine.

---

## 5. **لَا عَاصِمَ**

This is **لَا النَّافِيَةُ لِلْجِنْسِ**, the particle that completely negates the existence of something:

> **لَا عَاصِمَ الْيَوْمَ**

“There is absolutely no protector today.”

The noun **عَاصِمَ** is singular and accusative after this **لَا**.

---

## 6. **فَلَا تَسْأَلْنِ**

This means:

> Do not ask Me.

It consists of:

- **لَا** = prohibitive “do not”
- **تَسْأَلْ** = you ask, in the jussive
- **نِ** = Me; the protective nūn may be understood before the attached pronoun

The Qur’anic written form is concise.

---

## 7. Conditional jussive forms

In:

> **وَإِلَّا تَغْفِرْ لِي وَتَرْحَمْنِي أَكُنْ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ**

the verbs are connected to a condition:

- **تَغْفِرْ** = You forgive
- **تَرْحَمْنِي** = You have mercy upon me
- **أَكُنْ** = I will be

They are jussive because of the conditional construction.

---

## 8. **تَسْعَدُ** is not Form IV

The sentence means:

> **تَسْعَدُ الْأُسْرَةُ** = the family becomes happy.

It comes from:

> **سَعِدَ — يَسْعَدُ**

This is a Form I mujarrad verb from **بَابُ سَمِعَ**.

Do not confuse it with:

> **أَسْعَدَ — يُسْعِدُ** = to make someone happy, Form IV.

Compare:

- **سَعِدَتِ الْأُسْرَةُ** = the family became happy.
- **أَسْعَدَ الْوَلَدُ أُسْرَتَهُ** = the boy made his family happy.

# 7. خُطَّةُ الْحِفْظِ وَالإِتْقَانِ فِي يَوْمٍ إِلَى ثَلَاثَةِ أَيَّامٍ
## A One-to-Three-Day Memorization and Mastery Plan

> In one to three days you can gain strong understanding and short-term mastery. For lasting retention, review the material again after one week and one month.

---

## One-Day Intensive Plan

### Session 1 — Understand the argument, 35 minutes

Divide paragraph 7 into four ideas:

1. Parental love does not mean approving every mistake.
2. Noah invited his son to safety.
3. The son trusted the mountain instead of obeying.
4. Allah corrected Noah’s understanding of true family ties.

Read each part twice in Arabic and explain it in English without looking.

---

### Session 2 — Learn the Qur’anic sequence, 40 minutes

Memorize the story as a sequence of seven events:

1. **ارْكَبُوا فِيهَا** — board the Ark.
2. **يَا بُنَيَّ ارْكَبْ مَعَنَا** — Noah calls his son.
3. **سَآوِي إِلَى جَبَلٍ** — the son seeks refuge in a mountain.
4. **وَحَالَ بَيْنَهُمَا الْمَوْجُ** — the wave separates them.
5. **ابْلَعِي مَاءَكِ وَأَقْلِعِي** — the flood ends.
6. **إِنَّ ابْنِي مِنْ أَهْلِي** — Noah asks about his son.
7. **إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ** — Noah seeks Allah’s protection and forgiveness.

Retell these events orally from memory.

---

### Session 3 — Vocabulary, 25 minutes

Group the vocabulary by topic:

- **The Ark:** مَجْرَاهَا، مُرْسَاهَا، مَوْجٌ
- **Separation and danger:** مَعْزِلٌ، لَا عَاصِمَ، الْمُغْرَقِينَ
- **The ending:** الْجُودِيُّ، بُعْدًا
- **Building society:** لَبِنَاتٌ

For each word, recall:

1. its English meaning
2. its singular or plural
3. its Arabic synonym or explanation
4. one phrase from the passage

---

### Session 4 — Verbs and bābs, 40 minutes

Say each chain aloud:

- **أَوَى — يَأْوِي — اِئْوِ — إِوَاءٌ — بَابُ ضَرَبَ**
- **حَالَ — يَحُولُ — حُلْ — حَيْلُولَةٌ — بَابُ نَصَرَ**
- **غَاضَ — يَغِيضُ — غِضْ — غَيْضٌ — بَابُ ضَرَبَ**
- **رَكِبَ — يَرْكَبُ — ارْكَبْ — رُكُوبٌ — بَابُ سَمِعَ**
- **عَاذَ — يَعُوذُ — عُذْ — عَوْذٌ — بَابُ نَصَرَ**
- **رَحِمَ — يَرْحَمُ — ارْحَمْ — رَحْمَةٌ — بَابُ سَمِعَ**
- **سَعِدَ — يَسْعَدُ — اسْعَدْ — سَعَادَةٌ — بَابُ سَمِعَ**

Then separately memorize the derived forms:

- **أَقْلَعَ** — Form IV
- **نَادَى** — Form III
- **بَيَّنَ** — Form II
- **اسْتَوَى** — Form VIII

# 9. الخُلَاصَةُ
## Core Lessons to Remember

1. **True parental love includes guidance and correction, not blind approval.**
2. **Family connection does not remove personal responsibility before Allah.**
3. **Material means cannot protect anyone from Allah’s decree without His mercy.**
4. **A believer responds to correction by seeking forgiveness and refuge in Allah.**
5. **Healthy family relationships are founded upon righteousness, piety, cooperation, and moral responsibility.**
6. **Parents and children together form the building blocks of a righteous community.**
